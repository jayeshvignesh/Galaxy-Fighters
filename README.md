Galaxy Fighters

This is a two player game called Galazy Fighters is a thrilling shooting game. 

Join in on the fun here: https://jayeshvignesh.github.io/Galaxy-Fighters/
or click the link on the right of your page

Objective:
You are either a yellow or a red spaceship trying to take down the other spaceship by hitting it with your bullets 10 times
Best 2 of 3

Mechanics that might suprise you:
you can't have more than 5 bullets on screen per player

Controls:
NOTE: This doesn't use WASD to control one of the ships, as a keyboard shortcut is called when playing the game. 

Yellow Spaceship

E: Up
S: Left
D: Down
F: Right
L Ctrl: Shoot

Red Spaceship

Arrow Keys
R Ctrl: Shoot
